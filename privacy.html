1. Introduction

SureGut LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the SureGut Mobile application and suregut.com.

2. Information We Collect

As a health-focused nutrition scanner, we collect data that you voluntarily provide to tailor your experience:

  • Sensitive Personal Information: Self-reported health interests (e.g., renal or thyroid health goals) used to filter food scan results.
  • Device Data: Camera access (required for barcode scanning), IP address, and basic device identifiers for app functionality and crash reporting.
  • Subscription Data: We use RevenueCat to manage premium features. We do not store your credit card details directly; these are handled by the Google Play Store.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for:

  • Core app functionality (matching barcodes to your dietary rules).
  • Improving app performance and security.
  • SureGut LLC does not sell or share your health data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Under the 2026 CCPA/CPRA regulations, California residents have the following rights:

  • Right to Know: Request access to personal info collected since Jan 1, 2022.
  • Right to Delete: Request the deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Right to Limit: You have the right to limit the use of your "Sensitive Personal Information" (health data) to only what is necessary to perform the app's service.
  • No Dark Patterns: We ensure that opting out of data collection is as easy as opting in.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your dietary preferences as long as your account is active. You may delete your data at any time via the in-app settings menu. Upon request, all personal data will be purged from our systems within 30 days.
