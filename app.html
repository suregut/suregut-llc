Why it matters

Reading every label shouldn't be this hard.

Food restrictions are common and growing. An estimated 33 million Americans live with food allergies — about 1 in 10 adults and 1 in 13 children (FARE). Roughly 1 in 133 people, or about 1% of the population, have celiac disease (Beyond Celiac), and millions more manage kidney disease, thyroid conditions, IBS, and diabetes — diets that hinge on what's in the package.

For any of them, an ingredient list isn't optional reading — it's the difference between a safe choice and a flare-up. Yet the details that matter most (phosphorus additives, hidden gluten, high-FODMAP sweeteners, goitrogens) are buried in fine print and often don't appear on the nutrition panel at all. SureGut does that reading for you, against your specific profile, in seconds.

How it works

From barcode to verdict in seconds.

01

Scan

Point your camera at a product's barcode while shopping or planning meals. SureGut looks the item up in its product database, falling back to trusted food and nutrition data providers when it needs to fill in the details.

02

Analyze

SureGut reads the ingredient list and nutrition facts and checks them against every restriction in your active profile. When you combine conditions, it applies the strictest limit that applies — so a food has to clear all of your rules, not just one.

03

Decide

You get a clear result — Fits Profile, Does not Fit Profile, or Unknown Data when the label is incomplete — with the exact ingredients or nutrients behind the verdict highlighted, so you can decide before it goes in the cart.

FAQ

Common questions about SureGut

What dietary restrictions does SureGut support?

SureGut supports 50+ profiles spanning medical conditions (including chronic kidney disease, hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism, celiac disease, IBS/low-FODMAP, type 2 diabetes, GERD, gout, and hypertension), lifestyle diets (keto, paleo, Whole30, vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian, halal, kosher, and more), and major food allergens (peanut, tree nut, dairy, egg, soy, wheat, fish, shellfish, sesame, and others). You can combine several in one profile.

How does the SureGut food-label scanner work?

You scan a product's barcode with your camera. SureGut looks the product up in its database (falling back to trusted food and nutrition data providers when needed), then checks the ingredient list and nutrition facts against every restriction in your active profile. It returns a clear verdict — Fits Profile, Does not Fit Profile, or Unknown Data when the label information is incomplete — and highlights the ingredients or nutrients behind the result.

Is SureGut free?

Yes. SureGut is free with ads and includes 3 scans per day plus up to 5 additional scans after watching a short video. Premium removes ads and unlocks unlimited scans and up to five profiles for $1.99 per month or $19.99 per year.

Does SureGut work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. SureGut is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

Can SureGut check for more than one condition at once?

Yes. A single profile can combine multiple conditions, diets, and allergies. When rules overlap, SureGut applies the strictest limit, so a product has to satisfy all of your restrictions to earn a "Fits Profile" verdict.

Does SureGut provide medical advice?

No. SureGut offers informational support for food choices and does not replace medical advice. Always work with your physician or dietitian on your overall dietary plan.
