Delete Your SureGut Account

You can permanently delete your SureGut account and all associated data. The fastest way is from inside the app:

In the app: Open SureGut → Profile tab → Settings → Delete Account. Your account and data are removed immediately.

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion below.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in association (Apple / Google).
  • Your dietary profiles and saved conditions.
  • Your scan history.
  • Your daily scan-allowance records and any ad-attribution records.
This cannot be undone. Active subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google and are not cancelled by deleting your account — please cancel any subscription separately in the App Store or Play Store. Some records may be retained briefly in encrypted backups or where required by law (e.g., transaction records) before being permanently erased.

Request Account Deletion

To verify your request, please provide both your SureGut User ID and the email address associated with your account (from your Apple or Google sign-in). We will verify your request and delete your data within 30 days.

Where do I find my User ID? Open SureGut → Profile tab. Your User ID is shown near the top of the screen (it starts with auth_) and can be tapped to copy.

Prefer email?

You can also email us at support@suregut.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and we will process it within 30 days.
